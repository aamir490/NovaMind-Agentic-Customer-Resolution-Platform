Here is a realistic test case for your NovaMind Agentic Customer Resolution Platform. You can use it to test both the Customer and Admin workflows.

## 1. Customer — Create a New Case

**Subject:** Refund Not Received After Order Cancellation

**Description:**

I cancelled my order 7 days ago, but I have not received my refund yet. The payment was successfully deducted from my bank account, and the order status shows "Cancelled."

I contacted customer support earlier but have not received a clear update.

Please check my refund status and let me know when I can expect the amount to be credited back to my original payment method.

## 2. Admin — Request Message

Please review this customer refund complaint, analyze the issue, and recommend the most appropriate resolution.

Check the available case information, identify any missing details, and prepare a professional response for the customer.

If the refund status cannot be verified, clearly explain what information is required and recommend escalation to the appropriate support team.

Do not claim that the refund has been processed unless it is confirmed.

## 3. What to test in NovaMind

| Role     | Action                     | Expected behavior                 |
| -------- | -------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Customer | Create the refund case     | Case created successfully         |
| Admin    | Open the customer case     | Case details displayed            |
| Admin    | Submit the request message | AI analyzes the complaint         |
| Admin    | Review AI recommendation   | Suggested resolution is displayed |
| Admin    | Review customer response   | Professional reply is generated   |

Testing tip: Check whether NovaMind distinguishes between information provided by the customer and verified refund records. It should not invent refund status, transaction details, or completion dates.

Here is a realistic Laptop Damaged customer support case for testing your NovaMind Agentic Customer Resolution Platform.

## 1. Customer — Create New Case

**Subject:** Laptop Damaged and Not Working Properly

**Description:**

I purchased a laptop recently, but it has developed several problems. The screen flickers frequently, the laptop overheats during normal usage, and it sometimes shuts down automatically without any warning.

I also noticed that the laptop's outer body is damaged near the hinge, making it difficult to open and close the screen safely.

I would like to request an inspection and repair or replacement if the issue is covered under warranty.

Please review my complaint and guide me through the next steps to resolve this issue.

## 2. Admin — AI Request Message

Please analyze this customer's laptop damage complaint and recommend an appropriate resolution.

Identify the reported hardware problems, assess the possible severity, and determine what additional information is required to check warranty eligibility.

Suggest the next steps for technical inspection, repair, or replacement, and prepare a professional response for the customer.

Do not assume that the damage is covered under warranty or approve a replacement without verification.

## 3. Expected NovaMind Test Results

| Role     | Expected action                                                |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Customer | Successfully submits the laptop damage case                    |
| Admin    | Opens and reviews the submitted case                           |
| AI Agent | Identifies the screen, overheating, shutdown, and hinge issues |
| AI Agent | Recommends warranty verification and inspection                |
| Admin    | Reviews the recommendation before taking action                |

This example is useful for testing whether NovaMind handles hardware complaints carefully without inventing warranty decisions or promising replacements.
